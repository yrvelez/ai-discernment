# Reviewer pass (automated, single pass)

Model: `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5`. The report is mostly consistent with its tables, but it overclaims on the headline findings by ignoring multiplicity, misreports a scale, mislabels a pooled effect, and omits key deviation and robustness details.

- **high** — Key findings / H1: Three of 11 arm tests are called significant, and the key findings present Automated Flagging and Mindfulness as effects without any multiplicity correction. The p-values are 0.004, 0.032 and 0.043, so only Automated Flagging is even plausibly robust. The AI Literacy Guide result (p=0.043, CI lower bound 0.2) is fragile. The report also says 'no intervention reliably improved' accuracy, which conflicts with listing these effects as findings.
  - Suggested fix: Report Holm/BH-adjusted p-values for each hypothesis family. Describe the Mindfulness and Literacy Guide results as tentative and not robust to correction.
- **high** — E1-E3 robustness tables: The robustness and heterogeneity tables do not support the main results. In E2 (attention-check restriction), the full-sample estimates (Automated Flagging 0.032, Mindfulness 0.002, Literacy Guide 0.006) differ from the H1 estimates (0.044, -0.039, 0.047). Mindfulness and the Literacy Guide effects vanish. E1 and E3 also show no significant subgroup effects, apart from Automated Flagging in the low-familiarity group. The 'full' E2 specification evidently differs from H1 (probably no Lin interactions), and this is not explained or disclosed. The report never mentions that the significant H1 results are specification-dependent.
  - Suggested fix: State the specification difference explicitly. Explain why the E2 full-sample estimates differ from H1. Temper the H1 conclusions and discuss the E1-E3 results.
- **medium** — H3: The text says confidence is 'on a 5-point scale', but the section title and the outcome description say a single 7-point item. The confidence effects of -0.5 to -0.7 are also large for a 5-point scale. The summary calls the outcome a 5-point scale as well. Three of the H3 arm effects are uncorrected and include an unexpected positive effect (Inoculation), which is unexplained.
  - Suggested fix: Correct the scale description throughout. Report the effects in SD units or on the correct scale.
- **medium** — H2 / Key findings: The pooled attitude effect of -0.11 is called 'small but reliable', yet it is driven by one arm (Breathing Exercise, p=0.018) and the other arms are individually null. The pooling is also acknowledged to be approximate because the arms share a control group. The Key findings list it without that caveat. The SEs vary oddly across arms (AI Accuracy Nudge SE 0.276 vs. Provenance 0.128), which suggests heavy IPW or outlier influence.
  - Suggested fix: Drop 'reliable'. Carry the pooling caveat into the Key findings. Check weights and outliers.
- **medium** — Design and data: Several things a reader would need are missing: how the 227 exclusions were made (the n differs across outcomes, 2008-2030), how the IPW weights were built, whether the covariates were pre-registered, and whether the analysis plan was author-supplied. The 'Lin adjustment' and the random-effects pooling may be deviations from the registration, but every analysis is tagged with empty differences. The control group is also small (181) relative to the 11 arms.
  - Suggested fix: Document the exclusions, the weights, and any differences from the registration in the tags. Note the control-group size as a limitation.
- **medium** — Summary / H5: The exploratory finding that 'interventions improved detection of AI-generated posts but did not change recognition of authentic posts' is stated as a result in the Summary. The H5 pooled effect is +3.8 points and rests on uncorrected exploratory tests. The authentic-post (H6) results are not shown in the supplied text, which is truncated, so the claim cannot be verified. The Summary also doesn't note that a higher AI-detection hit rate with unchanged overall accuracy could just reflect a shift toward answering 'AI' (response bias).
  - Suggested fix: Label the claim as exploratory in the Summary. Show the H6 results. Report a signal-detection measure such as d' or the false-alarm rate.
- **low** — H1 text: The text rounds the Literacy Guide CI lower bound to 0.2 and describes the other arms as 'within ±2 points', but Breathing Exercise is +1.5 with a wide CI. The arm size n=78 for Inoculation is small, yet this is not flagged. The tables also show mean_treated for Mindfulness (0.696) that is higher than control (0.693), while the adjusted estimate is -3.9.
  - Suggested fix: Explain the divergence between raw means and adjusted estimates. Flag the small arms.
- **low** — Provenance: The report is marked FULLY AGENTIC with no human or reviewer pass, and the registration is described only as 'author-supplied'. This is not reflected in the confidence of the claims.
  - Suggested fix: Add a limitations note that the analysis was automated and unreviewed.
