- **high** — Summary / H1-H3: No multiplicity correction across 33 arm-level tests. Several 'significant' arms (Mindfulness p=.032, AI Literacy Guide p=.043 and p=.049, Infographic 2 p=.019) would not survive correction, and about 1-2 false positives are expected by chance. The Summary and per-hypothesis text treat them as findings.
  - Suggested fix: Report adjusted p-values (Holm/BH) or state explicitly that the individually significant arms are uncorrected and fragile. Lead with the pooled estimates.
- **high** — H3 / E2: The H3 pooled-row text says it was 'truncated in the provided table', yet the pooled value (-0.007, p=0.502) is given just above it. The E2 finding is cut off mid-sentence. E2 also says 'several arms show negative effects' on real content, but only one H3 arm is significant. E2 covers arms 5, 9 and 10 with no stated selection rationale.
  - Suggested fix: Remove the truncation sentence, complete E2, and justify the arm selection or include all arms.
- **medium** — E1: The interaction is described as 'interventions less effective for ChatGPT users'. The pooled treatment effect of 0.060 is for non-users, and the interaction of -0.061 implies roughly zero effect for users. This is a pooled, exploratory result that ignores that arms have opposite signs (e.g. Mindfulness is negative). The pooled H1 effect is 0.010, which does not match the 0.060 'any treatment' coefficient. That coefficient is a conditional effect for non-users, and the report does not say so.
  - Suggested fix: State that 0.060 is the effect among non-users. Note the heterogeneity across arms, and avoid causal language about 'knowledge gaps'.

Control mean is 0.693 in the text, but the intercept is 0.698 and the table lists mean_treated values that look odd. H1 says 'moderate heterogeneity' while tau²=0.0002. The Automated Flagging p-value is 0.0035 in the table and 0.004 in the text.

If the original plan clustered by participant on item-level data, collapsing to respondent-level scores changes the estimand. The mention of an 'interaction with media type' is also unexplained, since the models shown have no such term.
