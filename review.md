- **high** — E1 / Summary: E1 is used to say the Breathing gain is 'a shift toward labeling content AI-generated' and that the H2 gains are 'largely a criterion shift'. The evidence is one exploratory regression of the overall say-fake rate. It is not a sensitivity/bias decomposition (d'/criterion). The Breathing shift (+0.069) is smaller than its fake gain (+0.091). The finding also says Infographic 2's 'H2 fake-detection gains' are a criterion shift, but its H2 estimate (+0.055, p=0.138) is not significant. The Accuracy Nudge (E1 +0.037, p=0.092) and Guide (E1 +0.018, p=0.439) show no significant shift, so E1 does not support or refute a shift for them. The Summary states the Breathing attribution as an established finding.
  - Suggested fix: Soften to 'consistent with a partial response-bias component'. Drop the Infographic 2 H2 claim. Report a proper signal-detection analysis, or state that E1 cannot separate sensitivity from bias. Mark the Summary sentence as exploratory.
- **high** — Header / Registered versus implemented / H1–H3 headings: The report says no pre-registration exists and the plan was reconstructed post hoc. It still uses 'Registered direction', 'Registered versus implemented analyses', 'Planned results' and 'deviation from the original analysis plan' with a registered 'participant_id' cluster. This implies a binding prior plan. The 'deviation' is only a departure from a reconstruction of the original R script, and the tags are labeled as computed, not self-reported.
  - Suggested fix: Relabel as 'reconstructed plan (unregistered)', drop 'registered' wording, and say the deviation tag is relative to the original script, not to a pre-registration.
- **high** — H1–H3 / Summary: There are 33 arm-level tests plus 3 pooled tests, with no multiplicity correction. The headline 'above/below control' claims rest on borderline p-values: Mindfulness 0.032, Guide 0.043 (H1) and 0.049 (H2, CI lower bound 0.000), and Infographic 2 on H3 at 0.019. Under the roughly 5% false-positive rate across 33 tests, 1–2 nominal hits are expected by chance, and these would not survive Bonferroni or Holm adjustment. The Summary reports them without this caveat.
  - Suggested fix: Add adjusted p-values or an FDR column, and state in the Summary that only Automated Flagging (H1) and Breathing (H2) are robust to correction, if that is true. Otherwise present the others as tentative.
- **medium** — results/registered_summary.csv vs H1 table: Raw arm means and the adjusted estimates disagree in size and sometimes sign. Mindfulness: mean_treated 0.696 vs control 0.693 (raw +0.003) but estimate -0.039. Guide: raw +0.006 vs estimate +0.047. Breathing, Mindfulness and Inoculation all show an identical mean_treated of 0.696, which looks like a possible bug. The text never explains the gap, which could come from IPW, Lin covariate adjustment, or a computation error. The headline Mindfulness 'harm' and Guide 'benefit' depend on it.

The pooled SE is therefore too small, and the report itself concedes it is approximate. The Summary presents the pooled p-value with no caveat. The pooled estimate also averages interventions of very different kinds.
  - Suggested fix: Carry the shared-control caveat into the Summary. Use a contrast that accounts for the common control (e.g., a pooled treated-vs-control regression) as the primary pooled estimate, or downgrade the pooled test.
