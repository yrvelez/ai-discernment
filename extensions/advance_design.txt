Signal-detection replication of labels and literacy guide. 3 conditions: Control, Automated detector label, AI Literacy Guide. Primary outcome: Sensitivity d' and criterion c from a multilevel signal-detection model (probit mixed model of AI judgment on true item status, arm, and their interaction, with random effects for respondent and item).. Population: US adults from an online panel, about 420 per arm (1,260 total), with preregistered exclusions for failed attention check, speeding, and duplicate responses.
